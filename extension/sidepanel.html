Data & Privacy

Stays in your browser

  • Your prompts, queues and settings
  • Reference images you add. They go to Google Flow when a run uses them, never to AutoFlow's servers.

Sent to AutoFlow's servers

  • Your account: email address, and your password or Google sign-in
  • Usage counts for your plan's limits: how many prompts and runs, their mode and outcome, and Flow's ID for each generation. Not your prompt text or images.

Sent to Google

  • Your prompts and images, when AutoFlow submits them in your Google Flow tab, under your Google account
  • Only if you turn on LLM Diagnostics: Flow's error and tile status text, which can include a prompt, sent to Google's Gemini API with your own key
