AutoFlow Motion: privacy & data use

Updated October 11, 2026. Motion helps transform your selected videos using your own AI provider accounts.

What goes where

  • On your device: project settings, reference images, source videos and cut media, generated prompts, result links, upload receipts and login tokens are kept in extension storage or IndexedDB so work can resume.
  • AutoFlow account service: sign-in/registration details, account email, authentication tokens, extension version and requests needed to check subscriptions and reserve jobs. Job admission sends an identifier and SHA-256 fingerprint of inputs/settings, not the source video bytes. Server infrastructure can also process connection metadata such as IP addresses.
  • Your chosen AI service: Gemini, ChatGPT or Claude receives the task's selected media and instructions to analyze motion and write prompts. Google Flow receives video pieces, reference images and prompts to generate results. Their own privacy terms and retention settings apply. Submitted content can remain in those provider accounts after removal from Motion.
  • Whop: handles your existing AutoFlow Pro subscription. Motion is included with active Pro, with its own job allowance; there is no separate Motion purchase. The AutoFlow backend receives membership email, product/plan and membership/payment status events for Pro access checks. Payment card entry happens with the payment provider, not in this extension.

Browser access and consent

Motion reads and operates the supported provider tabs for your requested tasks, including uploads, prompts, progress and results. Automated video upload is off until you enable it. Uploading uses Chrome's debugger interface to control Flow's file chooser; Chrome displays its own debugging notice. Turning uploads off prevents new automatic uploads; it does not delete content already submitted. Keep the panel open while work runs.

Video link imports

A Motion icon appears on supported TikTok, Instagram and YouTube video pages and feed posts whose permalink can be identified unambiguously. It opens Motion and passes that selected link; it does not download or generate automatically. Selected links are held temporarily in browser session storage. Import starts only after you confirm ownership or reuse permission and click Import & download. With the default AutoFlow server method, your selected URL, permission confirmation and account access token go to our existing extractor service. That service downloads the public video using yt-dlp, sends the media to your extension, and removes its temporary copy after delivery, cancellation or a handled failure. No AI analysis or generation is started by importing. With Browser only, the selected page's downloadable media is fetched directly by your browser; no source video is sent to AutoFlow by that method. Media-host access is requested when needed. Both methods store the source locally and save a copy to Downloads. Your social-site cookies are never collected or exported to the server. Private or restricted media, site blocking, unavailable formats and protected streams may not import. Neither method removes watermarks or bypasses private-video or DRM access controls. Server import has a 100 MB / ten-minute limit and an abuse-rate ceiling distinct from generation allowances. Temporary storage is not a permanent library; unhandled server termination may leave temporary data until the server's ephemeral storage is cleared. Remove granted site access in Chrome's extension settings. Imported media goes to your chosen AI provider only if you later start a generation.

Your choices

Upload only media you have rights and permission to share. You can stop a run, disable uploads, sign out and delete saved projects from the project list. Deleting a project does not guarantee all cached media is erased; removing the extension clears its local browser storage. Downloads already saved on your device remain until you remove them. Manage or delete provider conversations and assets directly with each provider. Account, billing-record deletion and legal retention questions should be sent to the publisher's verified support contact on the store listing.

Limited use

AutoFlow Motion uses transferred user data only to provide or improve its single-purpose video workflow functionality, not to sell it, target advertisements or determine creditworthiness. Its use of data obtained through Chrome APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

This in-product notice does not replace the publisher's public privacy policy. Before public release, the publisher must publish and maintain a matching policy with an identified operator, support contact, retention periods and applicable user-rights process.
